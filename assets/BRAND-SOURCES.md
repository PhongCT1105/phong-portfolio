# Affiliation Asset Sources & Notes

This static package intentionally uses a mix of official local marks and neutral contextual icons. The moving affiliation strip is meant to communicate *relationship + recognition*, not imply employment or endorsement by every organization.

## NVIDIA
- Relationship: Incoming SWE
- Local asset: `assets/logos/nvidia.svg`
- Public logo source/reference: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/multimedia/corporate/nvidia-logos

## Adobe
- Relationship: Campus Ambassador
- Local asset: `assets/logos/adobe.svg`
- Trademark guidance: https://www.adobe.com/legal/permissions/trademarks.html
- If your Campus Ambassador agreement does not authorize corporate-logo display, change the entry in `js/content.js` to a neutral local icon.

## Runpod
- Relationship: Grand Prize / 1st Place
- Local neutral compact mark: `assets/logos/runpod-mark.svg`
- Official brand kit: https://www.runpod.io/brandkit
- Runpod's current styling is `Runpod` (capital R, lowercase p). The brand kit says not to recolor its official cube or imply partnership/endorsement.

## U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)
- Relationship: NSF REU / Research
- Local neutral research icon: `assets/logos/nsf-mark.svg`
- Brand/policy reference: https://www.nsf.gov/policies/brand

## UC Berkeley
- Relationship: Hackathon Recognition
- Local neutral trophy icon: `assets/icons/trophy.svg`
- Brand toolkit: https://brand.berkeley.edu/downloads/brand-essentials-toolkit/
- Defaulting to a neutral trophy avoids representing the personal portfolio as an official Berkeley property.

## Stanford
- Relationship: Hackathon Recognition
- Local neutral trophy icon: `assets/icons/trophy.svg`
- Identity guidance: https://identity.stanford.edu/visual-identity/stanford-logos/
- Stanford's identity guidance restricts use of Stanford marks on personal/non-Stanford projects, so this package uses text + trophy instead.

## IEEE / MIT URTC
